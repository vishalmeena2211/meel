# The data is not under the MIT licence

The code in this repository is under the MIT licence, in `LICENSE` at the top. The data in this folder is not: it keeps the licences of the places it came from, and those cannot be changed here.

| Data | Licence |
|---|---|
| Map data: positions, the line of each road, fuel pumps, the distances and towns in `site/route-approaches.json` | © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the [Open Database Licence](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). If you publish a changed copy, publish it under the same licence. |
| Heights | NASA's Shuttle Radar Topography Mission, served by Open Topo Data. Public domain. |
| Pictures in `images/` | Each under its own Creative Commons licence, with its author. Both are in `images/credits.json`, and must be shown beside the picture. |
| Pumps from the oil companies' locators (`pumps_from_companies` in `research/`) | Facts read from IndianOil's and HP's own outlet pages: a pump's name, town and position, each linking to its page. Neither site states terms of use; HP's says "All Rights Reserved". They are not Meel's to license: credit each to its page, as the route files do, and check the companies' terms before reusing them in bulk. |
| District codes in `site/route-districts.json` | The Local Government Directory's codes, read from Wikidata. CC0. |

The route files also hold facts gathered by desk research from official and published sources, each with a link to its source. Credit those sources as the route files do.

See [`README.md`](README.md), "Licences you must honour", for what each licence asks.
