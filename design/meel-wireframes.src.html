Meel wireframes

A phone-first website for Indian motorcycle touring, at rideplanner.in. One frame for each situation a rider can be in. “Meel” is Hindi for mile, as in the kilometre stones beside every Indian road.

Riders, shops, dates, distances and bike figures are example data. The example day is Monday 14 June 2027. The colours and type are a first proposal, not a decided brand.
