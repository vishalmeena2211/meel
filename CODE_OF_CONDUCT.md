# Code of conduct

Meel is for riders, and for anyone who helps keep its facts true. Everyone is welcome here: riders of every bike and every level of experience, people of every background, and people new to code.

## What is expected

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume the other person means well, and explain rather than scold.
- **Disagree about facts with sources, not with insults.** "This has changed, here is the notice" is how Meel works.
- **Keep other people's details private.** No names, phone numbers, addresses or chat group links of anyone who has not shared them here themselves.
- **Accept that the keeper decides** what goes in, and expect the keeper to say why.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or slurs, about anyone, for any reason: caste, religion, region, language, gender, or the bike they ride.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private details.
- Spam, advertising, and posting the same thing again and again.
- Encouraging dangerous riding.

## Where this applies

This repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone speaks for Meel.

## How to report

- **On GitHub:** use **Report content**, in the "···" menu of the comment, issue or pull request. It goes to GitHub's own team, who act on abuse anywhere on GitHub. Who reported it is not shared.
- **To the keeper:** the keeper reads every issue and pull request here, and hides or removes what breaks this code as soon as it is seen. If it needs to be said privately, open a [private report](https://github.com/vishalmeena2211/meel/security/advisories/new) and start its title with "Conduct:". Only you and the keeper can read it.

For a security problem, see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The keeper may edit or remove a comment, close or lock an issue, or block someone from the repository, for a while or for good, depending on how serious it is and whether it happens again. A first, small slip usually gets a private word.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), written in Meel's own plain words.
